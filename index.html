Sermon workshop
“A workman that needeth not to be ashamed” · 2 Tim 2:15

Prepare a sermon you wrote yourself, with a coach beside you

You do the study and the writing. The coach asks questions, checks your work against the passage, and keeps an eye on the clock. It won't write the sermon for you.

  1. Text & study. Choose your passage, read it closely, and write down what you see. Pull up background, key words and cross-references when you need them.
  2. Big idea. Say in one sentence what the passage means and what it means for your people.
  3. Outline & write. Build your introduction, points and conclusion. Ask the coach to review each part, or for a nudge when you're stuck.
  4. Review. Read the whole sermon through, get a full review from the coach, and save it.
Passage text and study help are written by Claude on request from public-domain translations. Check the wording against your own Bible, and treat explanations as a study partner's thoughts, not the final word.
